Thanksgiving Skiing in Colorado: What Is Open and When to Go

Thanksgiving is November 26, and half the Front Range is about to drive up I-70 at the same time. Arapahoe Basin, Copper Mountain and Winter Park are all spinning chairs, but only a few of them, so the wait at those lifts is where your holiday goes. What is open at A-Basin, Copper and Winter Park

In the PeakWait app today, Arapahoe Basin, Copper Mountain and Winter Park are all marked confirmed. That means the resort's own lift feed or skiers on the mountain show lifts running. They read lifts closed for the night right now, so treat this as where things stand, not tomorrow's snow report.

  • Arapahoe Basin: Open for the season since October 28. It started with the Black Mountain Express chair and the High Noon run, according to POWDER. The resort adds terrain as snow and snowmaking allow. I could not confirm what has opened since, so read its snow report before you drive.
  • Copper Mountain: Opened Sunday, November 8, two days later than planned because of warm weather. The American Eagle chair served Rhapsody, Fair Play and Main Vein, per Summit Daily. Copper said it would add more terrain as soon as it can.
  • Winter Park: Opened Friday, November 13 with first chair at 9 a.m. The Cabriolet, Meteor and Spirit lifts showed open on day one, per SnowBrains. The earlier 8 a.m. weekend start does not begin until Saturday, December 5, so Thanksgiving weekend still starts at 9.

Those are opening-day details, and each resort has had a couple of weeks to add to them. Use them to know what kind of day you are signing up for, not as a lift list.

The rest of the I-70 neighbors, and what still has not opened

Loveland is also marked confirmed in the app, and it opened Saturday, November 7, per Unofficial Networks. Keystone, Breckenridge, Vail and Eldora show open for the season on dates the resorts announced. Vail and Winter Park both opened November 13, according to CBS Colorado.

More is coming. Powderhorn and Purgatory list November 20. Beaver Creek and Crested Butte list November 25. Aspen Mountain and Snowmass list November 26. Those are all dates the resorts published.

Two things to double check

The app shows Steamboat at about November 22, but that is only the app's own estimate with nothing from the resort behind it. CBS Colorado reported on November 17 that Steamboat pushed its planned opening back to November 27. Check Steamboat's own site. Telluride, Monarch Mountain, Echo Mountain and Silverton Mountain are estimates only too, so do not book around them.

Wolf Creek is listed open for the season on a resort announcement. CBS Colorado says flooding temporarily closed it after its October opening day. Check the resort's site for its current status before you point the car that way.

Why the wait is at the bottom

With only a few chairs running, everyone in the parking lot funnels onto the same one or two lifts. Winter Park's mountain report listed four of 24 lifts and about one percent of the mountain open on opening day, per SnowBrains. One report from A-Basin's first weekend described the single chairlift as pretty crowded. That is the whole story of early-season lift lines. The resorts are not slow. The math is just brutal.

  • Be early: The first hour after the lifts open is when you can still get laps in before the crowd settles in.
  • Use the Rope Drop Club: At Winter Park, Ikon Pass holders who ski two days in November earn two Rope Drop days. Those give access to a separate, likely shorter wait at Super Gauge and the Gondola, per POWDER.
  • Eat at noon: Most people do, and the chair you were stuck at tends to thin out.

Which days of the long weekend are quieter

I do not have a headcount by day, so this is reading the calendar, not a measurement.

  • Thursday, November 26: Probably your best bet. AAA expects minimal road traffic on Thanksgiving Day, per WBUR. The skiers in the lot are the ones who skipped cooking, and there are fewer of them.
  • Friday, November 27: Everyone has the day off, and it shows. Expect the heaviest waits at the first chair, so get there before the lifts open.
  • Saturday, November 28: Copper hosts the men's World Cup super-G in East Village, and the races and events are free to attend, per Copper Mountain. Expect extra people around the base. If you want a calmer day, ski A-Basin or Loveland instead.
  • Sunday, November 29: The men's giant slalom is at Copper. On the hill, skiers who want to beat the drive home tend to bail early, which can help your afternoon. The drive home is the real problem, covered next.

When to leave Denver, and when to head home

Copper and Winter Park both start lifts at 9 a.m. that weekend. One road guide puts the westbound I-70 backup on winter weekend mornings at roughly 7 to 11 a.m., with Floyd Hill, Georgetown to Silver Plume, the Eisenhower Tunnel and Vail Pass as the choke points, per Arion. Holiday weeks act like an extended weekend. So for Friday, Saturday or Sunday, plan to be rolling out of Denver by about 6 a.m. and to be past that morning crush.

On Friday, the westbound window to avoid is the afternoon, roughly 2 to 7 p.m., per ski-season guidance summarized by Ski Lift Limo. If you are driving up Friday night for a Saturday start, that is the stretch to skip.

Coming home is the bigger problem. CDOT flags Sunday evenings between 3 and 6 p.m. eastbound, and says traffic can build as early as 11:30 a.m., per CDOT. AAA's national guidance for Sunday, November 29 is to drive before 11 a.m., and the worst window is noon to 8 p.m. That is national, not I-70 specific, but it points the same way. Either ski a short morning and leave by 11, or stay in the mountains for dinner and drive after the rush. CDOT recommends the dinner plan too.

What to actually do

  • Pick the day: Ski Thursday if you can. If you ski Friday through Sunday, leave Denver by about 6 a.m., be at the lifts at 9, and head east before 11 a.m. or after dinner.
  • Check before you drive: Read the resort's snow report and COtrip.org the morning you go, and confirm Steamboat and Wolf Creek on their own sites if they are in your plans.

FAQ

Are Colorado ski resorts open for Thanksgiving 2026?

Yes, several are. Arapahoe Basin, Copper Mountain, Winter Park and Loveland are confirmed running in the app, and Keystone, Breckenridge, Vail and Eldora show open on resort announcements. Terrain is limited, so expect a short list of lifts.

Which day of Thanksgiving weekend is least crowded for skiing?

Thanksgiving Day itself is the best guess. AAA expects minimal road traffic that day, and many skiers are home cooking. Friday and Saturday should be busier, and Copper has World Cup races on Saturday and Sunday.

What time should I leave Denver for first chair?

About 6 a.m. Copper and Winter Park start lifts at 9 a.m., and one I-70 guide puts the westbound weekend backup at roughly 7 to 11 a.m. Leaving by 6 gets you ahead of it.

Do I need a parking reservation at Arapahoe Basin over Thanksgiving?

The resort said reservations are only required on Saturdays and Sundays between January 2 and May 2, 2027. That puts Thanksgiving weekend outside that window, but check the resort's site before you go.

Is anything happening at Copper Mountain over the holiday weekend?

Yes. The Stifel Copper Cup brings a men's World Cup super-G on Saturday, November 28 and a giant slalom on Sunday, November 29. The races are free to attend and take place in East Village.

Will Steamboat be open Thanksgiving weekend?

Check the resort. The app only has an estimate of about November 22 with nothing from the resort behind it. CBS Colorado reported that Steamboat pushed its opening back to November 27.
