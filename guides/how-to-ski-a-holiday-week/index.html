How to ski a holiday week without spending it in lift waits

Christmas week and Presidents Day weekend are when a chair you barely notice in January turns into a long stand around. You can't make the crowds vanish, but you can stop spending your whole day in the worst of them. Here is what works, from someone who has stood in these waits and stopped doing it.

What a holiday week really is

The crush is not one day. The Breckenridge Nordic Center puts its busy stretch around Christmas and New Year's at roughly December 16 through January 2. CDOT says the heaviest I-70 volumes come around the holidays, school breaks and winter weekends. Presidents Day falls on Monday, February 15, 2027, so the weekend to worry about is February 13 to 15.

Here is a tell. The pass companies know where the crowds go, and they blackout those days on cheaper passes. The Epic Local Pass and the restricted Epic Day Passes are blocked December 26 to 31 and February 13 and 14. Ikon Base and Ikon Session are blocked December 26 to 30 and February 13 and 14 at most destinations. The full Epic Pass and the full Ikon Pass have no blackouts.

Notice what is missing. Monday, February 15 is not on those lists, so the restricted passes work again that day. Do not assume Monday is the quiet one.

Where Colorado stands today

It is November 12 and Christmas is six weeks away, so nothing here is a holiday problem yet. But the early-season picture tells you which resorts will be ready to absorb a crowd.

  • Confirmed running: In PeakWait today, Arapahoe Basin, Copper Mountain and Loveland show lifts running by the resort's own lift feed or our riders.
  • Announced dates: Vail has announced November 13. Beaver Creek, Crested Butte and Granby Ranch have announced November 25. Aspen Mountain and Snowmass have announced November 26. Aspen Highlands and Buttermilk have announced December 12.
  • Only projected: Steamboat, Winter Park and Telluride are our estimates, not resort dates, so check each resort's site.

Two flags. The app lists Breckenridge as open for the season, but a Colorado news roundup updated this week still showed its opening as pushed back from November 6. Check Breckenridge's site before you plan around it. News pages also list Steamboat at November 21, while our estimate is about November 22. Neither is proof of anything, so the resort's own site wins.

Beat the parking lot and the highway

On a holiday morning, your wait starts in the parking lot. Breckenridge tells guests to arrive before 8 AM or reserve a spot through its parking app. It also runs overflow lots with a free shuttle on busy weekends and holidays. Lodging in town says to reserve parking two months ahead for Christmas and New Year's, when you book the place.

Most Colorado resorts have their own version of this, so look up your resort's parking page before you leave, not in the car.

The road matters too. CDOT has described heavy Friday evening traffic and Sunday ski return traffic that peaks around midday on past holiday weekends. The I-70 Mountain Express Lanes are open on weekends and holidays, and they are toll only. During very high volume, CDOT may meter eastbound traffic at the Eisenhower-Johnson Tunnel. CDOT's Snowstang bus has run express to Loveland, Arapahoe Basin, Copper and Breckenridge over a past holiday weekend. Check whether it runs on your dates.

The schedule that works on a bad day

On a holiday, the mountain has a rhythm, and you can ski against it.

  1. Be on the first chair. The first hour after opening is consistently the calmest window, even on a busy day.
  2. Ski hard until about 11:30 on groomers that are still empty from roughly 8 to 10 AM.
  3. Eat lunch while everyone else is standing in lift lines, from about 11:30 to 1.
  4. Go back out around 1:15 when the crowd thins. Some skiers who show up around 1 PM report having a chair nearly to themselves by 3.

Saturday is usually the single busiest day, and midweek days run quieter. During Christmas week no day is truly quiet, but a Tuesday still beats a Saturday. This year the two Saturdays in the stretch are December 26 and January 2.

Pick your chairs, not just your day

The base area chairs are where the wait lives. Breckenridge is a good example. A local-minded review warns you off the Independence SuperChair on Peak 7 on weekends and holidays. It points to Kensho on Peak 6 and Falcon on Peak 10 as better bets. Another review calls the Quicksilver SuperChair and 5-Chair real chokepoints, because the resort has little green terrain elsewhere.

There is a catch. From Peak 9 or Peak 10, you have to take the Peak 8 SuperConnect to reach Peaks 6, 7 and 8. Get to the far side early, and plan your return before the afternoon flood.

The same logic applies to resorts. OnTheSnow has long pointed Presidents Week skiers toward lesser-known Colorado areas like Loveland. Its other advice is to find a section of the mountain a bit off the beaten path and to ski through lunch. A quieter resort on the same drive is worth considering.

What to actually do

Here is the short version. Do these three things and you will lose less of your holiday to the wait.

  • Plan the edges: Reserve or arrive early for parking, check your pass against the blackout dates, and leave the highway for off-peak hours.
  • Check before you commit: Open PeakWait at the base and read the reports from skiers on the mountain, rated Moving, Short, Busy or Long. Each lift shows how fresh the report is. It also shows which lifts are open and points you to the best next chair. Report the wait when you ride, since it is free.
  • Pick the resort by the day: PeakWait Plus ranks where to ski today across nearby resorts by snow and waits, which helps on the mornings when the first choice looks packed.

FAQ

What is the busiest day of Christmas week?

No source I found names one day. Pass blackouts run December 26 to 30 on Ikon Base and December 26 to 31 on Epic Local. Saturdays are usually the single busiest day, and this year those fall on December 26 and January 2.

Is Presidents Day weekend or the Monday worse?

OnTheSnow calls the weekend one of the busiest of the season. Restricted Epic and Ikon passes are blacked out February 13 and 14, but not Monday, February 15. Expect a crowd on all three days.

Do I need to reserve parking?

At Breckenridge, you should arrive before 8 AM or reserve a spot through the parking app. Overflow lots with a free shuttle run on busy weekends and holidays. Other resorts have their own rules, so check the resort's parking page.

Can I check lift waits before I leave?

Yes. Skiers on the mountain report each lift as Moving, Short, Busy or Long, and the app shows how fresh the report is. It is free to check and to report.

Is a midweek day in a holiday week quiet?

Quieter than a weekend, but not quiet. Tuesday through Thursday is the usual advice, and during Christmas week even those days are busy. Go early and ski through the midday rush.
