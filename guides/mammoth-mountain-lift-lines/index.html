Mammoth Mountain Lift Waits: The LA Weekend, Three Lodges and the Gondola

Mammoth sits about five hours from Los Angeles, and on a winter Saturday it can feel like most of LA made the drive. This guide covers where the weekend crowd piles up, how Main Lodge, Canyon and Eagle differ, and what the gondola to the top is really like. The resort announced Nov 13 as opening day, so we start there.

Opening weekend first: expect one front door

Mammoth has announced Friday, Nov 13 as opening day for the 2026-27 season. Treat it as a target. Last year the resort first aimed at Nov 14, moved it, and lifts did not start turning until Nov 20, according to SnowBrains. If the date matters to your plans, check the resort's own site the morning you drive up.

Early season is a different animal from February. A local snow report expects chairs 1 and 3 on opening day, with at least Broadway and Saddle Bowl. A second local shop says the early focus is Broadway Express and the base area around it. That means everyone who shows up on opening weekend is sharing a couple of lifts out of Main Lodge. Do not plan on the Canyon and Eagle tricks below yet. Visit Mammoth has noted that those two lodges typically open in mid-December, though that page is a few seasons old, so confirm on the resort site.

The LA weekend surge, and how it actually arrives

The drive from Los Angeles is roughly five hours up US 395, and Avant Ski puts it at about 315 miles. That distance does something odd to the crowd. Pine Forecast describes Southern California visitors bunching into Saturday morning arrivals, and says storms slow the drive and push arrivals later into the morning. Sunday return traffic on 395 can eat your afternoon even when the lifts were fine.

Here is what that means on the hill.

  • Friday night people: They sleep in town and are first on the lifts Saturday. You can be one of them.
  • Saturday late morning: The drivers arrive, park, boot up and hit the same few lifts at once. Main Lodge feels it first.
  • Sunday after lunch: Plenty of people leave early to beat the drive. Waits often ease while the sun is still good.

Powder weekends, Presidents' Day weekend and the winter holiday week are the heavy windows according to Pine Forecast. Midweek is calmer. If you can only go Saturday, the next sections are for you.

Main Lodge versus Canyon versus Eagle

Main Lodge: the big front door

Main Lodge sits at 9,000 feet and is the highest and longest running lodge, per the resort. It has direct access to the Panorama Gondola and the Main Park. It is also where the most people end up on a weekend. A Mammoth guide from Aso Mammoth says the lower mountain around Main stays crowded all day, and that around noon is your best shot at a run you really want there.

Canyon Lodge: the town side

Canyon is near town, and you can reach it on the free Village Gondola, which is how a lot of people with condos get there. It is central, and Eagle is one lift away. Visit Mammoth suggests skipping the wait at Canyon Express and taking Chair 8 early in the day. That tip matches a general rule from Aso Mammoth, which says express chairs often have longer waits than slower neighbors because everyone assumes they will be shorter. At Canyon, try Chair 8 before you commit to Canyon Express. Look at Chair 21 next to Gold Rush Express and Stump Alley Express too.

Eagle Lodge: the quiet side door

Eagle has gentle beginner terrain and access to the steeps of Lincoln Mountain, and it sits next to Juniper Springs Resort, per the resort. Visit Mammoth says Eagle and Canyon tend to have shorter waits than Main early in the day. From Eagle Express, Avant Ski points intermediates toward Chair 25 and Cloud Nine Express for glades, and calls the blue run Bridges rarely crowded. No lodge stays quiet all day, but Eagle is where I would start a Saturday if Main looked heavy.

The gondola to the top

The Panorama Gondola loads from the building across from the Main Lodge west entrance. The resort says the ride to the top takes about 7 minutes, and the summit sits at 11,053 feet. PeakWait tracks it as two pieces, Panorama Gondola Lower and Panorama Gondola Upper, each listed at 2,400 skiers per hour. The Village Gondola is a separate lift that runs between the Village and Canyon Lodge.

Two things make the gondola wait different from a chair wait.

  • Sightseers share it: The resort sells scenic rides on the same gondola, so you may be in a cabin with people holding coffee and no skis.
  • Weather holds: The resort tells you to check lift and trail status first, since the gondola can be on a weather hold.

Early in the season the top is not a given. One local snow report says the upper mountain needs a good three to four foot base to open. After a storm, expect a long wait at Chair 23 and a crowd at the top hunting for whatever powder is left, says Aso Mammoth. That same guide notes that the higher you go, the faster you usually get on a lift. So if the gondola wait is Long at 10 AM, ride it at noon when the first wave is eating.

Parking, shuttles and the 6 AM habit

Parking is free at Main, Canyon and Eagle, plus the Chair 4 and Chair 2 lots at the Mill, according to the resort. Lots typically open at 6 AM, and parking shuttles run at Main Lodge. SnoCal says there is no parking reservation system, and that overnight parking in winter lots gets you towed. That sounds relaxed until you remember it also means first come, first served on a Saturday.

The resort also points visitors to the free ESTA buses that connect town and all the lodges. If you are staying near the Village, leave the car and ride. Visit Mammoth suggests the first shuttle at 7 AM to reach Eagle or Canyon before the Main crowd builds, though that page is older, so check this season's schedule.

What to actually do on a Mammoth weekend

Here is the short version, from someone who has watched the Saturday 10:30 AM crowd at Broadway Express.

  • Beat the drive: Sleep in Mammoth Friday, or leave LA early enough to park before the late morning crush. Start at Eagle or Canyon once they are open, and take Chair 8 over Canyon Express when both are an option.
  • Check before you commit: Open PeakWait to see which lifts are open and the latest Moving, Short, Busy or Long reports, each with how fresh it is. Free to check, and the best next chair is right there.
  • Give back: When you ride out of a Long wait, report it from the same screen. It takes a few seconds, and the next skier at the gondola will thank you.

FAQ

When does Mammoth Mountain open for 2026-27?

The resort announced Friday, Nov 13. Dates can move, as last season showed, so check the resort's site before you drive.

Which Mammoth lodge has the shortest lift lines?

There is no permanent winner. Visit Mammoth says Eagle and Canyon tend to be quieter than Main early in the day. Main Lodge draws the biggest weekend crowd. Late in the season or around noon, things change, so check live reports.

How long does the Panorama Gondola take to reach the top?

The resort says about 7 minutes from the base area to the summit at 11,053 feet. It loads across from the Main Lodge west entrance. Weather holds can pause it, so look at lift status first.

Do I need a parking reservation at Mammoth?

SnoCal reports there is no parking reservation system. The resort says Main, Canyon, Eagle and the Chair 4 and Chair 2 lots are free. Arrive early on weekends, since lots typically open at 6 AM.

When is Mammoth the most crowded?

Pine Forecast lists powder weekends, the winter holiday week and Presidents' Day weekend as the heavy windows. Saturday morning is when most Los Angeles drivers arrive. Midweek and spring days are calmer.
