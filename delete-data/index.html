Delete your PeakWait data

PeakWait is an app by PeakWait LLC · Last updated: November 17, 2026

PeakWait has no accounts and no passwords. What we hold about you is tied to an anonymous identifier on your phone and, if you chose one, your PeakWait handle. You can delete it at any time, with the app or without it.

In the app

  1. Open PeakWait and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Your Data.
  3. Tap Delete my history, then Delete everything.

The deletion happens right away and cannot be undone. If you would like a copy first, tap Export my history on the same screen.

Without the app

Email contact@peakwait.net with the subject Delete my data and include your PeakWait handle, or write from the email address you added in Settings. We may ask a question to confirm the handle is yours. We delete the data within 30 days and write back when it is done. We use your email address only to answer the request.

If you never chose a handle and never added an email address in Settings, the app is the only way we can tell which records are yours, because we hold no name or phone number for you.

What is deleted

  • Your wait reports and lift observations that are linked to your device
  • Your ski day and season activity
  • Your handle and avatar, and your crew name if you added one
  • Your friend connections, friend requests, and mute settings
  • Any location you were sharing with friends, and your sharing settings
  • Your invite records and rewards, including free PeakWait Plus time you earned or were given
  • Your reporter reputation
  • Your analytics events and device state
  • Your email address if you added one, your problem and crash reports, and your answers to in-app questions

What we keep

  • The record that you agreed to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy: the version, the time, and the IP address used. We keep it for as long as it is needed to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.
  • Wait and lift reports that were stored with no link to your device. They cannot be traced back to you, so we cannot pick them out.
  • Totals and averages that no longer identify any device.
  • Anything the law requires us to keep for legal, security, tax, or anti-abuse purposes.

The full retention schedule is in section 4 of the Privacy Policy.

Good to know

  • Removing the app does not delete your data. Use one of the two routes above.
  • Deleting your data does not cancel a subscription. If you subscribed to PeakWait Plus on iPhone, cancel it in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. If you subscribed through Google Play, cancel it in the Google Play app under Payments and subscriptions.
