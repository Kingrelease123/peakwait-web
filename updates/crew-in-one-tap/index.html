1.7November 18, 2026

Your Crew in One Tap

Less setup in the parking lot, more time on snow. Here is what PeakWait 1.7 brings.

01

One tap and they're in your crew

Send your crew link from My Crew, or let a friend scan your QR code at the car. They tap it and they are in your crew. Nothing for you to find, nothing for you to accept.

One link is good for 30 days and 20 friends, so a single text covers the whole group chat.

02

Joining shares nothing by itself

Being in a crew does not put anyone on the map. Sharing your location on the mountain stays a separate switch, and each person turns on their own.

03

Crew requests find you

When someone asks to join your crew, a number now appears on the Crew tab the next time you open PeakWait. No more checking back to see who asked.

04

Starred mountains, in both places

Star a mountain and it sits under Favorites at the top. It also keeps its place in the full list, right where you expect to find it.

05

Also new this month

The How's the wait sheet now sizes itself to what it shows, so every wait band has room. And if PeakWait is earning its spot on your home screen, Settings has a new Rate PeakWait row.
