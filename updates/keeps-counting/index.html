A Day That Keeps Counting

Dead zones, cold hands and a crew to find. Here is what PeakWait 1.5 brings.

01

No signal, no problem

Mountains are full of dead zones, and your day now rides right through them. Losing signal no longer ends your day. It ends only when you actually leave the mountain, with every run and chair counted.

Open a resort with no signal and you see its lifts as they last were, with the time they were checked. Waits show as unknown until you're back online, because an old wait is not a live one. Any wait you tap is saved and sent the moment you have signal again.

02

How's the wait? Answer from your lock screen

Stand at a lift for a minute and PeakWait asks How's the wait? Tap Moving, Short, Busy or Long right on the notification, from your lock screen or your Apple Watch. No unlocking, no gloves off for long. If you'd rather not be asked, turn it off in Settings, Notifications.

03

A welcome when you arrive

When your day starts, PeakWait welcomes you to the mountain. Tap it and you're straight into that resort's lifts. Turn it on from the Day tab and the welcome reaches you in the parking lot, before you've even opened the app.

04

Your day, story-sized

Tap Share as a story after a day and PeakWait makes a full-screen picture of it: your vertical, runs, chairs and favourite lift. It's sized for Instagram and fits the group chat too.

05

Bring your crew, get a week of Plus

Invite a friend from the Crew tab. When they ski their first day with PeakWait, you both get a week of PeakWait Plus. Every friend adds another week, up to eight a season.
