Every Mountain, Right Now

One look tells you what a mountain is doing before you leave the house. Here is what PeakWait 1.8 brings.

Lifts spinning, at a glance

Open a mountain and a Right Now card tells you how many lifts are spinning, like 8 of 34. Under it sits the fact that matters most: when first chair went, or the latest change.

Each open lift says when it opened. A changes today button lists what opened, went on hold or closed.

Early mornings read clearly

Before first chair the card reads Not spinning yet and shows how many lifts ran yesterday. Lifts read Not open yet until they turn, and Closed today after last chair. Every time shown is the mountain's own, wherever your phone is.

Sort Resorts your way

Three buttons sit above the list: Most open, Nearest and New snow. Every card is two lines: the mountain and how far, then its lift count and pass. A blue snowflake marks new snow.

Drive time on the card

On iPhone, a mountain within 300 miles shows the drive time by car from where you are, marked with a small car. The time comes from Apple Maps and refreshes while you plan.

A faster mountain page

A mountain's page now draws its lifts first. The weather fills in right after, so the answer you came for is on screen sooner.

Notifications, your call

Settings has one switch for each kind of notification, plus Pause all. Two new notices join them: your day recap when a ski day ends, and opening day for a mountain you starred.
