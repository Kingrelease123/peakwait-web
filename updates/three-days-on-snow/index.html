A Sharper Ski Day

Your day on the mountain, counted the way you remember it. Here is what PeakWait 1.3 brings.

Every ride, every lift, your whole day

Ride the chair, ski the run, repeat. The Day screen keeps the score: each lift you rode, your vertical, and a recap ready to share when the lifts stop turning.

Your mark on the mountain

Standing in a wait? Tap it. Your report stays marked on that lift for 45 minutes, and once a second skier confirms it, everyone sees it. That is how the waits get on the map: skiers like you.

Metric, everywhere

Kilometers and meters on every screen, from your season stats to the leaderboards, if that is how you ski.

Any mountain, by its town

Type Bariloche and get Cerro Catedral. Type Squaw and get Palisades Tahoe.
