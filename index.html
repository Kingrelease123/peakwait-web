Questions

Good to know before your first chair.

Anything else? Write to contact@peakwait.net.

Is PeakWait free?

Yes. Live waits, lift status, the best next chair, your day, your crew and reporting are all free. PeakWait Plus adds the planning tools for $34.99 a year, with 7 days free to try.

Where do the waits come from?

From skiers on the mountain. They share a wait in two taps, and phones standing in a wait time it on their own, so waits stay live even when someone forgets to report.

Why bands and not minutes?

Moving, Short, Busy and Long show what a crowd of skiers can reliably tell. When there is not enough to go on yet, PeakWait shows the lift's status and a typical wait for that hour.

Does it work with no signal?

Your day keeps counting with no signal, and anything you report sends the moment you are back in coverage.

Which mountains?

116 across North and South America, Epic, Ikon and independents. You can request a mountain right in the app.
